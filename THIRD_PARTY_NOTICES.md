## Dictionaries

Honmani ships no dictionary data. Users import their own or download them from their publishers'
own release links from within the app: Jitendex (https://jitendex.org, CC BY-SA 4.0) and JMdict
for Yomitan (https://github.com/yomidevs/jmdict-yomitan; JMdict is EDRDG-licensed, CC BY-SA 4.0). Each dictionary's attribution from its
`index.json` is stored and should be shown with its entries.

## Radical search data

`app/src/main/assets/radicals.txt` is derived from RADKFILEX/KRADFILE (which kanji contain which
radicals) and KANJIDIC2 (stroke counts, frequency rank) of the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (https://www.edrdg.org, licence: https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html,
CC BY-SA 4.0). `scripts/radicals/build_radicals.py` makes it; the radicals RADKFILE draws with a
substitute kanji are mapped to their own Unicode forms. The file is shared under the same licence.

## Example sentences (optional download)

Not part of the app. When the reader switches on Settings → Example sentences, the app downloads
a SQLite file made by `scripts/examples/build_examples.py` from Tatoeba (https://tatoeba.org)
exports: Japanese sentences with English translations and the word index of the Tanaka corpus
(`jpn_indices`). Sentences are © their contributors, licence CC BY 2.0 FR
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/). The file is published as a release asset of
https://github.com/cnfsd-labs/honmani-reader (tag `examples-1`) under the same licence.

## Interface sounds

`app/src/main/res/raw/ui_*.wav` are made by `scripts/sounds/make_sounds.py` from plain sine tones
(no samples, nothing third-party); they share the app's licence, GPL-3.0-or-later. They only play
when the reader switches on Settings → Appearance → Sounds.

## Free books catalogue

`docs/aozora/index.json` is generated by `scripts/aozora-index` from the Aozora Bunko work list
(https://www.aozora.gr.jp/index_pages/person_all_extended.html), CC BY 4.0.
It lists only works Aozora marks as free of copyright. The texts the app downloads from
https://www.aozora.gr.jp are public domain works (https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html).

The optional difficulty (`d`) of each work in that index is approximate and computed ahead of time
with the app's own difficulty measure from two dictionaries: the Jiten word frequency list
(https://jiten.moe, CC BY-SA 4.0) and Jitendex (https://jitendex.org, Stephen Kraus, CC BY-SA 4.0,
with JMdict and Tatoeba inside). The numbers are derived from them, so they carry this credit;
none of the dictionaries' own data is published.
